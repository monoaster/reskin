# Blueprint

Draw the scene as a cyanotype technical drawing in the 1000 x 1000 world. Paper is a radial gradient from #0f4fb0 to #072b6c with faint grain, a fine grid every 20 units and a coarse grid every 100. All linework is white #eef9ff at 2 px, with no fills except a 1 px hatch. Add a sheet frame with zone ticks, a title block lower right (SHEET 02/10, SCALE 1:20, REV C, live time, beat, status) and a parts list lower left. Draw the room in elevation: double window frame with a city skyline and hatched crescent moon, clock with a beat-ticking hand, desk, laptop with real code text, a plant that hides the desk lines behind it, and centre lines. The figure is outline construction: hatched capsules, dashed far limbs, joint circles with cross-hairs, a dash-dot spine. Dimension lines in #9fe6ff (1.5 px, 1 world px = 4 mm) follow the rig: knee and elbow arcs with degrees, stride, height. At 2.8 the screen fills pale with SHIPPED, a red-orange #ff5a2e APPROVED stamp slams in at -8 degrees with a short shake, and confetti are drawn circles, crosses and triangles.
