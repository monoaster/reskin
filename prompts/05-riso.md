# Risograph

Print three inks, fluorescent pink #ff48b0, blue #0078bf and yellow #ffe800, on cream paper #f3ead7 with fibre grain. Draw each ink as its own greyscale coverage canvas at 500x500, where grey means how much ink. Run a halftone pass with typed arrays: a cosine dot screen per ink at its own angle (pink 75, blue 15, yellow 45 degrees, cells of 5 to 6 px), solid where coverage is near 1. Add density blotches and dropout specks. Multiply the three inks onto the paper, then scale up to w x w. Offset the layers by about 2 px at this size in different directions, wobbling slowly with t, and jolt the blue plate when the build passes. No outlines: flat shapes and dots, with a paper-coloured keyline around each body part and a 30 px paper margin with registration marks. Wall: blue dots. Window sky: blue under pink dots. Floor: yellow under pink dots. Desk: navy. Plant: yellow with blue dots. Hoodie: solid pink. At 2.8 the screen flips to solid pink with a crooked paper SHIPPED stamp, yellow halftone rays burst from the laptop and thin out, and confetti pieces get a paper underlay and one ink each.
