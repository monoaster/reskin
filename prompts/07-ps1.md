# PS1 Low-Poly

Write a small software 3D renderer and draw the scene as a 1997 console would. Camera: fixed, high, a little off axis, 58 degree field of view. Render into a 160 x 160 buffer with a z-buffer, then scale up with `imageSmoothingEnabled = false`. World x = rig x, height = 800 - rig y, z = joint.z * DIMS.depth. Round every projected vertex to whole pixels so edges crawl. Light only at vertices (Gouraud): ambient, a ceiling lamp, moonlight from the window, and the laptop screen, which turns green on ship. Fade vertices to a dark purple fog. Sample textures with nearest filtering and no perspective correction, and cut floor and walls into big cells so textures visibly warp. Then quantise to 5 bits per channel with a 4x4 Bayer dither. Build the character from boxes and hex prisms (hoodie, jeans, white sneakers, headphones around the neck) and turn the head 35 degrees toward the camera so its pixel-painted face reads. Room: wallpaper, skirting, rug, doorway, night-city window, clock, desk, laptop, plant, blob shadows. On ship: a check on the screen, spinning quad confetti, a pixel-font SHIPPED banner (5x7 glyphs, 2x) and a blinking SAVING... memory-card icon.
