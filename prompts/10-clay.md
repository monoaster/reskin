# Clay Stop-Motion

Make everything plasticine, shot on threes. Quantise t to 1/8 s on a grid that includes 2.8, so the build passes on a held frame. Every object is a lumpy blob: a capsule or rounded shape with a low seeded wobble on its outline. Fill it with a radial gradient lit from the top left, then add a soft crescent shadow on the far side, a dark inner rim with a light lip, a small specular dot, and a fingerprint and dent texture (cached, applied with soft-light). Limbs are separate capsules with visible joins. On every step give each object a fresh offset up to 1.6 px, a tiny rotation, and a 1 to 3 percent squash or stretch. Shadows are blurred, fall to the lower right, and sit under the feet. Palette: wall teal #6fa8a6, table #dcbc86, hoodie orange #f08a2d, jeans #3d62ad, skin #f1b48f, desk #a6683c, yellow #f5cf3c, cream, green #3fbf6a. The wall is painted cardboard with brush strokes. At 2.8 swap the screen for a green clay slab with pressed SHIPPED letters, throw clay balls and lumps stretched along their path, and squash the character into a crouch, a tall stretch and a flat landing.
