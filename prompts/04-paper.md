# Paper Cut-Out

Draw a shadow box of layered craft paper. Every object is a flat polygon cut from one sheet: kraft #cf9f64, cream #f4e7cb, coral #ee6b56, teal #2f9a95, mustard #eab13e, navy #1f2a52, plus peach and denim. Give each outline a deckled edge: split edges into 10 px steps and nudge each vertex about 1.2 px, seeded per object. Every sheet casts a soft shadow, light from the top left; nearer layers get longer, blurrier shadows (wall 9, desk 10, laptop 12, character 14). Lay a fibre grain (noise plus short fibres, soft-light) over everything, inside a sloped kraft box frame 40 px wide. The window is a hole in the wall with navy sky, paper city, stars and a cream moon on a tab. Build the character from rigid pieces (head, hair, hoodie, arms, thighs, shins, shoes) on brass brads, and move it on twos: quantise t to 1/12 s and add a tiny seeded wobble per frame. Confetti and screen stay smooth. At t=2.8 a green sheet slides down over the screen, a tick and a SHIPPED label pop in on a spring with tape, a starburst opens behind the laptop, and the confetti are cut paper scraps with shadows.
